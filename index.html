Our Story
2019 Founded

Luxury is not
worn — it is felt.

Lataj was born from a quiet conviction: that clothing should move with intention, speak without words, and endure beyond season. Each piece is a study in restraint — cut in rare textiles, finished by hand, conceived for those who understand the difference between fashion and dress.

We work with a closed circle of ateliers across Milan and Marrakech, sourcing only materials that can carry a story. No excess. No compromise.
